Deal hacks

Practical ways to compare offers and make your budget go further.

Shopping tips

1.Look beyond the crossed-out price

Compare the current price with other sellers and recent prices. A large advertised discount alone does not tell you whether an offer is good value.

2.Check price history

Price history can help you see whether a sale is unusual or repeats often. Compare the same model, size and condition.

3.Check open-box conditions

Read the condition grade, included accessories and return policy. Inspect and test the item when it arrives, while you can still return it.

4.Review coupons and subscriptions

Check whether discounts can be combined and confirm the final total at checkout. For Subscribe & Save or similar offers, review future prices, delivery dates and cancellation terms.

5.Read the price-match policy

Check which competitors and products qualify, what proof is required and whether there is a deadline. Policies can change.

6.Check for store offers

A retailer may offer an email sign-up discount or a saved-cart reminder. Compare any offer with the current sale and check its exclusions.

7.Compare cashback and rewards

Check cashback eligibility, exclusions and payment timing before relying on it. Card fees or interest can outweigh rewards.

8.Use discounted gift cards carefully

Check the seller's guarantee and the card's terms. Buy only what you plan to use, and consider how refunds would be handled.

9.Watch seasonal clearance

Seasonal products may be reduced as stores change their ranges. Compare prices and availability rather than assuming every clearance label is a deal.

10.Compare sizes and colors

Prices can vary between options on the same listing. Check the exact option you want and confirm that it qualifies for the advertised offer.

11.Review refurbished warranties

Check who refurbished the item, what was tested and who provides the warranty. Compare the warranty and return policy with a new item.

12.Respect purchase limits

Follow the retailer's quantity limits and buy only what you will use. A limit does not by itself mean the price is unusually low.

13.Plan purchases ahead

If you have flexibility, compare prices outside peak demand. Include storage needs and the return window when buying well in advance.

14.Set a spending limit

Decide what you need and what you are willing to spend before shopping. Include shipping, taxes and any required extras in your comparison.
