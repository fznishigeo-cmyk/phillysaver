ⓘ About Review Checker

Every great find starts with a little curiosity. Review Checker gives you a friendly overview of the rating and review signals available for an Amazon product: an established review base, a well-received rating, steadiness over time, review pace and recent popularity.

Our grades are A, B and C. A means a strong set of signals; B means helpful signals to explore; C means okay — read a few reviews before you buy. Limited data can also lead to C. We show only the checks a product passes, with a plain explanation of each.

Make it part of your shopping routine. Use {{SPOTTER_ABOUT}}the Deal Checker to explore today's price and verified deals. Then read a few reviews on Amazon to see how the product fits your needs.

Review Checker is free, works on any device and needs no sign-up. We look at available signals, which may be incomplete or older than today's price. We don't read review text, detect fake reviews or adjust Amazon's ratings.
