ⓘ About Review Checker

Reviews are one of the best parts of shopping on Amazon. But a star rating on its own doesn’t tell the whole story.

How long have the reviews been building up? Has the rating held steady, or did it change quickly? Are people still buying it today? Those are the review signals that are hard to see at a glance.

That’s why we made Review Checker. Paste an Amazon product link and we look at the review signals behind the product, then give you a simple A, B or C grade with the checks it passed.

How it works:

  1. Paste a link. Copy the Amazon product page link (or its 10-character ASIN) into the box. Typing a product name instead opens Amazon’s highly rated list.
  2. We check five review signals: an established review base, a well-received rating, a steady rating over time, a natural review pace and recent popularity.
  3. You get an A, B or C grade, with the checks the product passed and a plain explanation of each.

Our grades are A, B and C. A means a strong set of signals; B means helpful signals to explore; C means okay — read a few reviews before you buy. Limited data can also lead to C.

What we don’t do: we don’t read review text, detect fake reviews or change Amazon’s ratings. Signals can be incomplete or older than today’s price, so it’s always worth reading a few reviews too.
