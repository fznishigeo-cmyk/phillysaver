Work with us

We are tiny. That is not false modesty; it is a headcount.

wowpriced is a small crew and a lot of code. There is no HR department, no open requisitions, and no ping-pong table. What we have is a deal wall that refreshes every 10 minutes, a membership that pays for the servers, and more good ideas than hands. If one of the roles below sounds like you, we would like to hear from you, even if it is just to say hello.

People we would love to hear from

Deal scouts

You already live in the clearance aisle. You know which warehouse listings to refresh and when. You can tell a real markdown from a sign in about two seconds. We want to turn that instinct into tips that feed the wall, with credit and a thank-you that is more than a thank-you.

A designer

The wall is plain on purpose, but plain and good are different things. We want someone who can make a grid of price cards feel calm and obvious on a phone, in light mode and dark mode, without adding a single pop-up.

A writer

Short, warm, honest, and a little funny. No hype, no "you won't believe." If you can explain anchor pricing to a friend in three sentences and make them laugh once, you can write for us.

A part-time developer

Python, a few hours a week. The engine is a polite crawler, a verifier that checks every price against a word-for-word quote, and a static site. We care about tests, small diffs, and honoring robots.txt more than we care about frameworks.

How to reach us

Write to us through the contact page. Tell us which role, show us one thing you have made or found, and skip the cover letter. We read everything and reply to all of it, though sometimes it takes a few days, because, again, tiny.
