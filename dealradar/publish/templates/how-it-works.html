How to use wowpriced

One page, a few controls, and no homework. Here is the tour.

The wall in ten steps

  1. Start with the tabs. The row under the header splits the wall into departments: Home & Kitchen, Electronics, Toys & Games, and so on. "All" shows everything.
  2. Try the Used & Open-Box tab. This one is different. It is not a department. It pulls together every deal, from any department, where the item is open-box, refurbished, or used. Warehouse listings and returned stock live here, and the discounts tend to be the deepest on the site.
  3. Search for what you want. The search box matches the item name, the brand, and the store. Type "air fryer" or "Ninja" or "Target" and the wall narrows as you type.
  4. Pick a store. The store dropdown lists every store with a live deal right now. The list rebuilds itself as deals come and go.
  5. Sort it your way. "Fresh drops" is the default: the newest hour of arrivals first, re-dealt every cycle so the top rows keep moving. You can also sort by biggest percent off, most dollars saved, cheapest first, or newest first.
  6. Set a minimum percent. Only want to see 70% off and up? Type 70 in the "min %" box. Everything below it hides.
  7. Tick "2+ sources only." This hides any deal that only one source has reported. What is left has been seen by at least two independent sources, or is backed by our own price history.
  8. Read the badges. Every deal on the wall passed the word-for-word price check and the fake-list-price critic. The badge tells you how it was confirmed on top of that: ✓ 2+ sources means independent sources agree. ✓ price history means the price is well below what we have recorded for that item before. FAQ

Why was a deal gone within minutes?

Because the good ones are good. A 70% off price error or a deep clearance markdown gets found by a lot of people at once, and stock runs out. We re-check prices and mark dead deals GONE as fast as the {{DROP_MINUTES}}-minute cycle allows, but we cannot hold inventory for you. Fresh drops and email alerts are the answer.

Why do some cards show a colored store tile instead of a product photo?

We only show images we have the rights to show. When the seller provides a public product image, you see it. When they do not, you get a tile with the store name instead. We would rather have a plain tile than borrow a photo.

Why are prices quoted word-for-word?

Because a quote can be checked and a summary cannot. Every price on the wall comes from a verbatim snippet of the source, and a program confirms the snippet contains that exact number before anything is published. If the check fails, the deal is dropped, not guessed.

What does "open box" mean?

An item that was sold, opened, and returned, then inspected and put back on sale at a lower price. The box might be damaged. The item usually is not. Sellers grade condition and state what is included, so read the listing. Open-box items often come with a shorter return window than new, which is worth knowing before you buy.

What does the "min %" number mean?

It is the percent off at checkout, before any rebate. If a deal depends on a rebate to hit its price, the card says so.
