Shoppers: report a dead deal

Amazon prices are re-checked live about every three hours, other stores' deals are marked GONE when the source says so or the deal expires, and the wall itself republishes every 10 minutes, so a deal can still die between checks. If you clicked through and the price was gone, send us the item name and the store and we will mark it GONE. If the price was never real, tell us that too; it helps the fake-list-price critic get sharper.
