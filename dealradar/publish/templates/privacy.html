Privacy

What we collect, how we use it and the choices available to you.

The short version

We keep what it takes to run your account: your email address and, once you subscribe, a reference to your payment record. We never see your card number.

We use your email only to send deals. Joining free needs only an email address. We never see your card number.

You can turn off advertising and session-recording services below, and they stay off if your browser sends Global Privacy Control.

Your account

  • Email and password. We store your email address and a protected version of your password, never the password itself.
  • Third-party sign-in. If you choose this option, we receive your email address and an account identifier, never your password from that provider.
  • Either way, the account record also holds when it was created, whether you have used the free trial, and your payment customer ID once you subscribe.

Addresses on the earlier email list are kept until you unsubscribe or request deletion. We are not currently sending deal emails to that list.

Payments

Our payment processor handles checkout and billing and tells us whether your membership is active or in its trial. We never see or store your card number. The processor's privacy policy covers its payment records.

Your email address

When you join free, we store your email address, sign-up time and confirmation status to manage the deals email. Deal emails are starting soon; we will send a confirmation email first. You can unsubscribe anytime or ask us to delete your address.

If you use third-party sign-in, we also keep your email address, the provider's identifier, sign-up time and verification status in a sign-in record. This lets us verify your sign-in. You can ask us to delete this record too.

Support payments

Our payment processor handles optional support payments, including any recurring payments you choose. We never see or store your card number. The processor keeps payment records under its own privacy policy.

Service providers and sharing

We use service providers that host the site and send email, process payments, support sign-in, and provide analytics and advertising services. They receive the information needed to provide those services. Hosting providers receive your IP address, browser type and page requests to deliver and protect the site.

Analytics, cookies and advertising

When enabled, analytics and advertising services may use cookies and similar technologies to measure site use, improve the site and measure or show our ads.

  • Analytics measures visits, page views and actions such as deal clicks. It remains active when you opt out below, with advertising features and ad personalization disabled.
  • Session recording records clicks, scrolls and page views to help improve the site. Typed text and account details are masked. It does not load when you opt out or send Global Privacy Control.
  • Advertising partners measure visits from ads and sign-ups, checkouts, trial starts and deal clicks.free joins, support clicks and deal clicks. They do not load when you opt out or send Global Privacy Control.

These services can see the page address and browser information. Our action reports include the store and category of a clicked deal, never your email address or the item's name or price.

Saved-list actions send only a count and a fixed action label to our analytics provider. We never send your shortlist, deal identifiers or saved labels in these events. The Saved view is masked from session recording.

Affiliate links

When you follow an affiliate link, the retailer or affiliate partner may use cookies or similar technology to attribute a purchase and pay a commission. Their privacy policies apply on their sites. See our affiliate disclosure.
